# Project Bullseye — Prelaunch Advertising Plan

Status: **PRELAUNCH / OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**  
Last updated: **16 August 2026**<br>
Public production: **NO-GO**  
Paid campaign destination: **waitlist or dedicated prelaunch landing page only**

## Objective

Build a qualified audience for NASH AI Markets without presenting example data as live, implying guaranteed results, or encouraging customers to trade a regulated product. The first campaign should sell the **preparation process and product experience**, not an outcome.

## Campaign position

> A calmer way to prepare for the S&P 500 session.
>
> Verified context, clear risk conditions and a repeatable decision process — including the confidence to stand aside.

NASH AI Markets is an educational market-analysis and decision-support product. It does not execute orders, hold customer funds or provide personalised financial advice.

## Launch sequence

### Phase A — organic prelaunch, allowed now

- Publish the approved Bullseye launch teaser.
- Produce 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 edits with burned-in subtitles.
- Post founder-led product walkthrough clips showing the real Bullseye UI with prominent example-only labels.
- Drive traffic to a waitlist page, not a paid checkout.
- Measure visits, completed waitlist requests and qualified replies.

### Phase B — small paid waitlist test, only after compliance preflight

- Confirm whether the campaign and landing page are financial promotions in each target jurisdiction.
- Complete any platform advertiser or financial-services verification required.
- Obtain qualified review of ad copy, landing page, risk wording and intended targeting.
- Run one small UK test with a fixed budget cap and no automatic budget expansion.
- Optimise for a completed waitlist form, not clicks, video views or broker referrals.

### Phase C — paid subscription acquisition

Do not begin until:

1. the public launch gate is cleared;
2. the marketed data capability has written display/redistribution rights;
3. the complete signup, Checkout, webhook, membership, email and onboarding lifecycle has fresh evidence;
4. monitoring, rollback ownership, privacy/retention and financial-promotion approval are recorded;
5. the advertised landing page matches the product customers actually receive.

## Recommended creative angles

### 1. Discipline over impulse

**Hook:** Stop chasing every move.  
**Body:** Build the session plan first: context, catalysts, risk, confirmation and no-trade conditions in one Bullseye workflow.  
**CTA:** Join the launch list.

### 2. See the session. Know the risk.

**Hook:** The chart is only one part of the session.  
**Body:** Bring S&P 500 context, volatility, yields, catalysts and decision conditions into one calm preparation screen.  
**CTA:** Preview the experience.

### 3. Waiting is a decision

**Hook:** Not every session deserves a trade.  
**Body:** Bullseye makes incomplete evidence and stand-aside conditions visible before impulse takes over.  
**CTA:** Join early access.

### 4. One repeatable morning routine

**Hook:** Open. Orient. Prepare.  
**Body:** A structured Morning Brief and Trading Desk designed to make the next decision clearer, not louder.  
**CTA:** Get launch updates.

## Approved wording principles

Use:

- educational market analysis
- market preparation
- decision support
- verified context
- example-only or delayed data, where applicable
- risk conditions
- wait for confirmation
- no-trade conditions
- process, discipline and review

Avoid:

- guaranteed or risk-free
- beat the market
- make money
- AI predicts the market
- winning trades
- trade signals
- proven profits
- accuracy or win-rate claims without audited evidence
- profit screenshots
- fake urgency or invented scarcity
- luxury-lifestyle imagery
- broker, spread-betting or CFD calls to action
- any example screen that could reasonably be mistaken for current live guidance

## Mandatory footer for campaign landing pages

> Trading and investing involve risk. NASH AI Markets provides educational market analysis and decision-support tools, not personalised financial advice. Example screens may contain illustrative or delayed information and must not be treated as live trading instructions.

The final wording remains subject to qualified UK financial-promotion and advertising review.

## Measurement

The credit- and privacy-saving organic default is now explicit:

- use existing server-side valid waitlist-completion totals as the sole conversion
  metric;
- record platform post reach manually from the platform's own dashboard;
- do not add video-start, video-complete, form-start, behavioural-session or
  advertising-pixel scripts merely for prelaunch;
- add first-party landing-session attribution only if a later qualified review
  and approved measurement purpose show it is necessary;
- email-delivery, activated-account and paid-membership measurement remains off
  until each underlying operation is separately launch-cleared.

Do not upload customer lists to advertising platforms without an approved privacy basis and vendor-register entry.

## First paid-test decision rule

A paid test is ready only when all five answers are **yes**:

- Is the advertiser/platform verification position confirmed?
- Has qualified review cleared the ad and landing page?
- Is the destination public, accurate and conversion-measurable?
- Is the marketed capability available to the visitor today?
- Is there a fixed spend cap and named owner who can stop the campaign immediately?

Any **no** means remain in organic/waitlist mode.
