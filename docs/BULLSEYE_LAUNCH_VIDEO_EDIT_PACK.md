- Burn subtitles into every social export; also retain the matching `.srt` file.
- Use at most two lines at once and keep each line short enough to read once.
- Use a solid or strongly shaded dark backing plate, not outline-only text.

- Never place subtitles over the example/not-live disclosure.
- Use UK spelling and punctuation exactly as written in the supplied files.
