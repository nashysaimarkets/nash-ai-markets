## One-line position

> Bullseye makes market preparation clearer by showing what is verified, what is
> missing and when waiting is the disciplined decision.

## Non-negotiable publishing rules

1. Sell the process, interface and founder story—not a market outcome.
2. Never claim signals, accuracy, profits, a win rate, certainty or permission to trade.
3. Keep example-only, delayed, stale and unavailable labels readable for the whole shot.
4. Never show a broker account, P&L, customer record, email address, secret or private-preview token.
5. Use only the deterministic Bullseye preview. Do not call a live market-data provider to make content.
6. Do not scrape, download or republish TradingView charts or data. Use an official embed only after its terms, attribution and commercial use are confirmed in writing for the intended placement.
7. Do not start paid promotion until the owner has recorded qualified UK financial-promotion review and platform approval.
8. Use this footer whenever a caption or screen could be understood as a product promotion:

> Educational market analysis and decision support only—not personalised financial
> advice. Trading and investing involve risk. Example screens are not live trading
> instructions.

## Lean measurement and remix rules

Use only each platform's native aggregate analytics at first. Record no viewer
identity and buy no analytics service.

Track per post:

- three-second hold rate;
- average percentage viewed;
- saves and shares per 1,000 views;
- qualified comments or direct questions;
- profile visits;
- later, public waitlist completions only after that connection is approved.

After the first seven days:

1. Keep the top two hooks by hold rate.
2. Keep the top two explanations by saves/shares.
3. Recut each winner into a new 6–12 second version with a different first frame.
4. Change only one variable at a time: hook, length or screen—not the compliance wording.
5. Stop any asset that creates repeated misunderstanding about signals, live data or guaranteed outcomes.

## Zero-credit production workflow

1. Capture the protected deterministic preview once in 16:9 at high resolution.
2. Record the four product areas as separate 8–20 second source clips.
3. Crop those same originals into 9:16 and 1:1; do not re-run providers or regenerate market fixtures.
4. Save reusable end cards, subtitle style and limitation footer as editing presets.
5. Record all founder scripts in one phone session beside a window with a quiet background.
6. Use platform-native scheduling, captions and analytics where available.
7. Keep a local content log; add a paid tool only after organic evidence justifies it.

## Final pre-post checklist

- Private-preview token and browser address bar are cropped out.
- **EXAMPLE ONLY / NOT LIVE** remains readable wherever figures appear.
- Subtitles are accurate and the product appears in the opening two seconds.
- Caption and audio contain no signal, profit, certainty or performance claim.
- Music, logo, type, footage and every visible asset have commercial-use rights.
- Risk/product footer is present.
- Destination is approved, mobile-tested and accurate on the day of posting.
- A named person is ready to moderate misleading claims in comments.

## Basis for the creative rules

- The [FCA's social-media financial-promotion guidance](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-warns-firms-and-finfluencers-keep-their-social-media-ads-lawful) stresses that promotions must be fair, clear and not misleading, with balanced risk presentation.
- The [FCA's April 2026 enforcement update](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-spearheads-global-action-stop-illegal-finfluencers) shows that illegal finfluencer activity remains an active enforcement priority.
- [TikTok's official creative guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads) supports native vertical production, an early hook, captions and a clear hook/body/close structure.
- [YouTube's official Shorts guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12921536) supports a repeatable upload workflow and consistent publishing.

These sources guide creative execution; they are not a substitute for qualified
legal review of the final promotion, audience and destination.
