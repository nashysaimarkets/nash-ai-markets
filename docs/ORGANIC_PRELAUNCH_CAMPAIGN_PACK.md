## Editing rules

1. Keep videos subtitle-first; many viewers watch without sound.
2. Put the product on screen within the first two seconds.
3. Show one idea per clip rather than scrolling the entire site.
4. Keep example/delayed labels visible for the entire shot in which the figures
   appear.
5. Do not zoom into an illustrative price, percentage or confidence score as the
   principal hook.
6. Do not add green-profit counters, cash sounds, broker P&L screens, luxury
   imagery or claims of customer results.
7. Use the approved Bullseye pulse and restrained soundtrack; avoid alarm-like
   noises or false urgency.
8. End with the logo, one value line, the waitlist CTA and the standard footer.

- the caption contains no profit, prediction, signal or certainty claim;
