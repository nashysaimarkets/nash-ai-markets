# Pocket Bullseye — Professional Standard & Conversion Benchmark

Date: 2026-10-04

## External benchmark

### Professional benchmark: TradingView
- UK App Store: 4.8/5 from roughly 49,000 ratings (checked 2026-10-04).
- Standard to borrow: chart-first hierarchy, restrained visual language, dependable interaction, predictable controls, clear data provenance, no decorative motion competing with the chart.

### Direct screenshot-AI benchmark: Trade AI: Chart AI Analysis
- UK App Store: 4.5/5 from roughly 1,300 ratings (checked 2026-10-04).
- Standard to beat: simple upload-to-answer flow, clear takeaways, saved history/follow-up continuity, obvious product value before complexity.

Small-sample apps with 4.7–4.8 ratings are useful references but are not treated as stronger evidence than products with hundreds or thousands of ratings.

## Pocket non-negotiables

1. Evidence report opens first after every scan.
2. Decorative cinematic/radar sequences never block the result.
3. Main scanner rail is limited to six jobs: Chart X-Ray, Patterns, Liquidity, Scenarios, Plan, Risk.
4. Every scanner communicates VERIFIED, PARTIAL or NO EVIDENCE.
5. Numeric chart prices appear only when the price scale can support them.
6. Pattern claims pass deterministic geometry checks after model output.
7. Liquidity claims are screenshot-derived references only. Never claim hidden stops, institutional order flow or order-book liquidity.
8. If evidence is weak, fail closed. Empty is better than invented precision.
9. Motion is allowed only when it improves chart orientation, focus or spatial understanding.
10. No cartoon animals, shields, rockets, game badges, neon theatre or novelty animation in the primary analysis path.

## Next App Store positioning

Do not lead with 3D, cinematic mode or novelty.

### Suggested subtitle
AI Chart Second Opinion

### Suggested opening
One chart. One honest challenge.

Upload a trading chart and get an evidence-led second opinion before you decide. Pocket Bullseye checks visible structure, support and resistance, pattern geometry, liquidity references, contradictions and reasons to wait — and withholds precision it cannot verify.

### Screenshot order
1. SEE WHAT POCKET VERIFIED — verdict + Scanner Health
2. VERIFIED LEVELS — scale-checked support and resistance
3. PATTERN WATCH — no forced pattern labels
4. LIQUIDITY GUARD — visible pools and sweeps, no hidden-order claims
5. WHAT DID I MISS? — strongest contradiction
6. DECISION PLAN — what confirms, what fails, when to wait

## Conversion rule

Do not change price, free-analysis allowance or subscription terms without an explicit commercial decision. Reliability and trust are improved first; free-use experiments should be measured against AI cost and conversion.
