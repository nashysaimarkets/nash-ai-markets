### Description

Pocket Bullseye is a mobile-first chart-reading assistant built to help traders slow down, challenge a setup and see what they may be missing.

Upload a clear chart screenshot and receive a structured second opinion covering visible market structure, support and resistance, bullish and bearish scenarios, invalidation evidence, pattern context, setup quality and reasons to wait.

Key features:

- One complete chart analysis free
- Visible support and resistance mapping
- Bullish, bearish and wait scenarios
- Pattern Watch and chart-quality checks
- Setup score, risk flags and invalidation evidence
- Optional higher-timeframe comparison
- Reanalysis and user correction tools
- A clean, mobile-first result experience

Pocket Bullseye provides educational, conditional decision support. It does not execute trades, connect to a brokerage account, provide personalised financial advice or guarantee outcomes. Always verify information independently and manage your own risk.

An active monthly subscription is required after the free analysis. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings.

## App review notes

Pocket Bullseye analyses screenshots supplied by the reviewer. It does not place trades, connect to a broker or display live executable prices.

Review flow:

1. Create or use the supplied review account.
2. Select a clear chart screenshot with an instrument label, timeframe, candles and price scale.
3. Confirm the on-screen Privacy Shield.
4. Select LONG, SHORT or JUST ANALYSE and run the analysis.
5. The first completed analysis is free. A second new analysis presents the native Apple subscription screen.

The app uses the photo library or camera only after the reviewer chooses to add a chart. Account deletion is available in the signed-in profile. Restore Purchases is available from the subscription screen.
