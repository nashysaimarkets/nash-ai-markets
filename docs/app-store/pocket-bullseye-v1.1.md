## Subtitle

AI Chart Analysis & Plans

## Promotional text

Challenge your chart before you trade. Pocket Bullseye checks visible structure, verifies price levels, tests the opposing case and saves each decision for a later process review.

## Screenshot sequence

1. Upload one chart — Get an evidence-first second opinion.
2. Trust Gate — Exact prices appear only when the chart scale passes verification.
3. Bull vs Bear — See both cases before money meets market.
4. Decision Map — Know what strengthens, breaks or invalidates the read.
5. Private Journal — Save today’s reasoning and review it against a later chart.
6. Trader Fingerprint — Turn repeated decisions into useful personal patterns.

## What’s new in 1.1

Support, resistance and Level Lab scans are now more reliable across different chart sizes and price-scale layouts.

Liquidity Guard has improved calibration and clearer evidence handling. Pattern Watch now lets you switch between supplied 30-minute, 1-hour and 4-hour chart views.

This update also improves scanner recovery, safe navigation, modal controls and subscription-screen accessibility. Unverified levels remain withheld and partial evidence reduces confidence instead of filling gaps with guesses.

## Rating prompt rule

Ask for an App Store rating only after a customer has confirmed an accurate result or completed a later-chart process review. Never interrupt the first analysis or ask immediately after installation.
