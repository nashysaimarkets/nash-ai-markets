**Full description (4,000 characters maximum)**

One chart. One honest challenge.

Pocket Bullseye is an evidence-first second opinion for traders who want to slow down, test their bias and see what the chart may be saying before they act.

Upload a chart screenshot and Pocket Bullseye examines only the evidence visible in your image. It maps structure, challenges both sides of the setup and turns the result into a clear decision-support story.

WHAT YOU GET

• A plain-language setup grade and verdict
• Support, resistance and current-price context in one Decision Map
• Bull-case and bear-case evidence shown side by side
• Pattern X-Ray for visible formations and confirmation conditions
• Liquidity Guard to highlight chart areas that deserve extra care
• Chart preflight checks for instrument, timeframe and price
• Cinematic results plus a detailed written report
• A private Decision Journal stored on your device

BUILT FOR A BETTER PROCESS

Pocket Bullseye does not connect to your brokerage account, place trades or promise outcomes. It is designed to challenge a plan—not validate a trade. The app can tell you when evidence is incomplete, when opposing scenarios remain credible and when patience may be the strongest decision.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Remove names, account numbers, balances and notifications before uploading. Your selected chart images are sent securely to our configured AI provider only to produce the audit you request. Saved decisions remain in local device storage and are not synchronised to a NASH AI Markets account.

TRY IT FIRST

The native app includes one completed chart analysis. Continued access is available through a monthly Google Play subscription at the localised price shown before purchase. Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play and use Restore Purchases when moving to another Android device.

IMPORTANT

AI can misread or omit chart information. Pocket Bullseye provides general educational decision support, not personalised financial advice, a recommendation or a trading signal. Always verify the instrument, timeframe, price and chart levels against your original broker chart. Trading involves risk and losses are possible.
