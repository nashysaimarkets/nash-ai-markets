## FAQ copy

**Does Pocket Bullseye tell me what to buy?** It provides educational chart context. You make your own decisions and should independently check every finding.

**Is the example a real scanner result?** No. It uses synthetic candles and predefined explanations. No AI, market-data request or upload is made by the example.

**Why can a review stay on HOLD?** A price axis, instrument or timeframe may be unconfirmed; evidence may be insufficient; or uploaded views may conflict. HOLD is an explanation of a limit, not proof a trade will fail.

**Can the app see hidden orders or predict a market?** A screenshot shows visible chart information. A suspected sweep zone does not establish actual resting orders. Findings do not guarantee future prices.

**Can I connect IG or place orders?** The existing analysis app does not place orders. A separate IG DEMO read-only pilot is in preparation and is not a public partnership or a live dealing feature.

**Is it free?** The proposed release requires a paid subscription for real scanner analysis; the synthetic example is free to view and uses no scan credits. Final wording must match the accepted signed app and web policy before publication.

**How do I restore an Apple purchase?** Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that purchased it. If access still fails, contact support without sharing passwords or payment-card details. Signed-device acceptance remains a release gate.

**What image should I upload?** Use a readable chart showing the instrument, timeframe and price axis. Add views of the same instrument only. Avoid private account information in screenshots.
